Accessibility & non-discrimination

Karts2Me aims to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Our apps support screen readers, keyboard navigation, visible focus, zoomable text, and reduced-motion settings.

Riders with disabilities

Service animals are welcome on every ride. Drivers may not refuse a ride or charge extra because of a disability, a service animal, or a mobility device that can be safely carried. If a cart cannot accommodate a rider's need, the operator must help arrange an alternative where one exists.

Non-discrimination

Karts2Me prohibits discrimination based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, disability, or any other protected status, by riders, drivers, and operators.

Report a problem or request help

Email support@karts2me.com with the page or screen and what went wrong. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
