Questions

Good to know before you start.

What kinds of properties use Karts2Me?

Resorts and golf clubs, campuses, stadiums and venues, HOAs and managed communities, and event grounds: anywhere carts or shuttles move people inside a bounded area.

Do I need my own carts and drivers?

You bring the fleet. For drivers you can add your own team or approve gig drivers who apply through the platform.

How does pricing work for riders?

You set base, per-seat and per-mile pricing, and can choose to let riders bid at your property. Riders see the price before they request.

How do payments and payouts work?

Rider payments and driver payouts run through Stripe, so you don't handle card data or manual payouts.

How long does setup take?

Creating your account, adding your zone and fleet, and approving drivers can be done in a single sitting. Tell us about your property and we'll help you launch.

What about insurance and licensing?

Operators are responsible for the insurance, permits and regulatory requirements that apply to their property and vehicles. Talk to us if you have questions about getting set up.
