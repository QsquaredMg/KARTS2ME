Questions

Good to know before you start.

Where can I use Karts2Me?

At any resort, campus, stadium, community or event where the property has turned Karts2Me on. Sign up, and the app shows you what's available near you.

How much does it cost?

Signing up is free. Ride prices are set by each property and shown before you request. Some properties let riders bid on the rate.

How do I pay?

In the app by card, processed securely by Stripe. You can split the fare with the people riding with you.

Are the drivers vetted?

Drivers apply, submit identification, and are approved by the property before they can accept rides.

Can I bring a service animal or mobility device?

Yes. Service animals are welcome on every ride, and drivers may not refuse or add charges because of a disability. See our accessibility page for details.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from inside the app at any time. Your personal data is removed as described in our Privacy Policy.
